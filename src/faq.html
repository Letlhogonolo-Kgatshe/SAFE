About SAFE

Money conversations, as casual as the weather

SAFE helps novice investors grow and learn. Financial literacy should be as common as talking about the weather, so we package essential information in one place, tailored for beginners and beyond.

We're not reinventing the wheel, just delivering clear, practical knowledge. Our vision: to make money conversations as casual as asking "How's your portfolio looking?" or "Seen any new listings?"

Built by Letlhogonolo Kgatshe as a solo entry (Team Arctic) for the South African Intervarsity Hackathon 2025.
